About

I am an AI Engineer & AI Product Builder focused on researching, designing, and shipping practical intelligent systems using Generative AI, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and modern full-stack web architectures.

Currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Galgotias University. What sets me apart is my 7+ years of professional experience in digital design & illustration, with over 3,000+ published commercial assets across Adobe Stock, Freepik, and Shutterstock. I don't just train algorithms in notebooks; I bridge complex machine intelligence with thoughtful human interfaces to create products people actually love using.
